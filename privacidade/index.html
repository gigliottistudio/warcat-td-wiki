Warcat TD: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 (game version 0.1.29)

Developer: GT Studio

Contact: contato@gigliottistudio.com.br

Warcat TD is a single-player tower defense game. This page explains what the game stores on your device and what it sends over the internet.

Short version

  • The game works fully offline. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-game purchases.
  • The Daily Challenge also works offline: the day's match is built on your device from the date, and the result stays on it.
  • Nothing is sent unless you say yes. The first time you open the main menu the game asks whether it may send anonymous usage data. If you say no, or don't answer, nothing leaves your device. You can change your answer at any time in Options.
  • We never ask for your name, email, or any other personal detail.

What stays on your device

Your settings (volume, language, accessibility options) and your progress (unlocks, records, achievements, the Daily Challenge history and saved matches) are saved in local files in the game's user data folder (opcoes.cfg, progresso.cfg, progresso_run.cfg and progresso_diario.cfg). In the browser version they are saved in your browser's local storage for the game's page. They are never uploaded. Deleting the files, or clearing the site data in your browser, erases them.

What is sent if you say yes

If you allow it, the game sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog (PostHog Inc., US cloud, us.i.posthog.com), to help us balance and fix the game. The events are:

  • Every event: game version, platform (web or Windows), interface language, and a random session code.
  • Opening and closing the game, and a signal once a minute while the game is open (whether you are in a match, the mode and the wave).
  • Starting and ending a run: mode (Run, Campaign or Daily Challenge), chapter, difficulty, hero, omen, starting hand, result (victory, defeat or abandoned), wave reached, lives lost, towers built, upgrades, relics, duration.
  • Each wave you clear: lives lost, which monsters got through, which towers you built, gold, duration.
  • Boss kills, Convocation choices and achievements unlocked.
  • In the Daily Challenge: every match event also carries the challenge date and whether it is the attempt that counts or practice.

We do not send your name, email, files, or anything typed by you.

Anonymous ID. Events carry a random number generated on your device and kept in your settings file. It does not identify you, and the events are sent without a person profile ($process_person_profile: false). It only lets us count how many different devices play and whether they come back. If you erase your settings, a new number is created.

IP address. Like any internet request, the one that carries these events reaches PostHog's servers with your IP address. PostHog stores it with the event, and we use it only to estimate your country and city. We do not use it to identify anyone.

Who receives it

PostHog acts as our data processor and stores the events in the United States. We do not sell the data, share it for advertising, or combine it with other sources. Event data is kept for 1 year; after that PostHog deletes it.

Stores and platforms

If you play on itch.io or buy the game on Steam, those services collect their own data under their own policies (for example page views, downloads, purchases and your Steam account). The game does not read your Steam or itch.io account for analytics.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop sending: turn off Send anonymous usage data in Options. The game stops immediately and discards anything waiting to be sent.
  • Delete data already sent: write to the contact above with your anonymous ID (the telemetria_id value in opcoes.cfg) and we will delete the events linked to it. Without the ID we cannot tell which events are yours, because the data is anonymous by design.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the Brazilian LGPD) you may have further rights, such as access and deletion. Use the same contact.

Children

The game is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. Because it asks for consent before sending anything, a parent or guardian can simply answer no.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and describe the change in the game's release notes.
